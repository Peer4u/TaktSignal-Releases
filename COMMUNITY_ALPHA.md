# What “Community Alpha” means

TaktSignal Community Alpha is offered free of charge so that people who run ERPNext can evaluate it and tell us what
works and what does not.

- **Early software.** It has been through extensive automated, synthetic and internal testing, including labs against
  real ERPNext 15 and 16 sites with synthetic data, but **not** yet through enough real factories. Alerts can be
  wrong, late or missing.
- **Decision support only.** TaktSignal highlights possible risks; you remain responsible for production, purchasing
  and delivery decisions. Check its findings against ERPNext and the shop floor.
- **Read-only.** TaktSignal only reads from ERPNext. Setup refuses Administrator and accounts that ERPNext reports as
  able to write. Use a dedicated reader account.
- **Your data stays with you.** It runs on your computer; its database and backups are local. No telemetry.
- **Start safely.** Use a staging, isolated or test copy of ERPNext first, and keep backups.

Who it is for: manufacturers and ERPNext implementers who can evaluate a new tool carefully and give feedback.
